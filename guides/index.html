Fantasy Football Injury Guides

Injuries decide more fantasy matchups than almost anything else, and most of what you need to know comes from a handful of official reports. These guides explain what each one means, when it comes out, and what to do with your lineup.

  • NFL Injury Designations Explained: Questionable, Doubtful, Out and IRWhat questionable, doubtful, out, IR, PUP and NFI actually mean for your fantasy lineup, when each one is announced, and how to act on it.
  • How to Read NFL Practice Reports: DNP, Limited and FullWhat did-not-participate, limited and full practice mean, why the Wednesday-to-Friday trend matters more than any one day, and how estimated reports work.
  • Game-Day Inactives: The 90-Minute Window That Decides Your LineupWhen NFL inactives are released, how to use them with questionable players, and how to set up your lineup so a late scratch doesn't cost you a week.
  • Fantasy Football Handcuffs: Who to Roster Behind Your StartersWhat a handcuff is, which positions and situations make one worth a roster spot, and how to keep track of the players whose injuries change your team.
  • Planning Around Bye Weeks in Fantasy FootballHow NFL bye weeks work, how to spot the weeks that will hurt your roster, and when to start making waiver moves so a bye never leaves an empty lineup spot.
  • Fantasy IR Slots vs. the NFL's Injured ReserveHow fantasy football IR slots work on ESPN, Sleeper and Yahoo, which injury designations make a player eligible, and how to use the slot to gain a roster spot.
