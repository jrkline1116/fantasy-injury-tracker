How to read this report

Q
Questionable. Uncertain to play. Most questionable players do suit up, so this alone isn't a reason to bench someone. Watch the Friday practice line and the inactives list 90 minutes before kickoff.
D
Doubtful. Unlikely to play. Treat him as out and have your backup ready. The rare doubtful player who does play is often limited.
O
Out. Will not play this week. Bench him and pick up a replacement if you need one.
IR
Injured reserve. Out for at least several weeks. If your league has an IR slot, move him there to free a roster spot.
SUS
Suspended. Not injured, but not eligible to play until the suspension ends.

Practice participation

Teams report each player's practice status on Wednesday, Thursday and Friday (earlier in Thursday-game weeks). The chips next to each player show those days in order:

DNP
Did not practice. A DNP on Wednesday is common for veterans and isn't alarming by itself. A DNP on Friday is a strong sign he sits.
LP
Limited. Did part of practice. Limited all week usually ends in a questionable tag and a game-time decision.
FP
Full. Did the whole practice. A player who finishes the week with a full session almost always plays.

The trend matters more than any one day. DNP → LP → FP means he's getting healthier as the game approaches. FP → LP or LP → DNP late in the week is a warning sign, even if the final designation is only questionable.

When things change

Final designations come out with Friday's report for Sunday games (a day earlier for Thursday and Saturday games, and Saturday for Monday night). The last word is the inactives list, released about 90 minutes before each kickoff: a questionable player who isn't on it is playing. This page refreshes on its own every two minutes while it's open, and a player who just changed is highlighted.

Where the data comes from

Designations come from public NFL injury reports and team depth charts, checked every 2 minutes within 3 hours of a game and every 15 minutes otherwise. Practice participation is read from each team's daily report. Players who are fully healthy aren't listed. Only fantasy positions are shown; team defenses are covered on our sister site, Fantasy Defense Assist.
