What it watches for you

Linked players

Every receiver is linked to his quarterback and every running back to the back behind him. A QB ruled out means a heads-up about your WRs. A starter ruled out means an upgrade for your handcuff.

League sync

Link your ESPN (public or private) or Sleeper league and your roster stays in sync: trades, pickups, and lineup moves. League-mates join with one invite link. Yahoo is coming soon.

Pre-game check

One summary before each kickoff window, timed how you like, listing any starter who is out, doubtful, or still questionable, right after inactives come out.

Tuesday waiver report

Every Tuesday morning: your hurt players and next week's byes, each with a suggested backup, marked available or taken in your league.

If/then rules

"If he's active, start him over my other guy." Set it once and get one alert with the exact swap when the news drops.

Quiet when it should be

Quiet hours, practice reports once a day, no noise during games except a starter getting hurt, and Monday news held for Tuesday.

How it works

  1. Sign in with your email. No password: we send a 6-digit code.
  2. Add your team. Link your ESPN or Sleeper league, or type your roster in by hand.
  3. Turn on notifications. That's it. Statuses are checked every 2 minutes near game time.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, every alert type, with unlimited teams. The website shows a small ad, and there's a Buy Me a Coffee link if you want to chip in.

Which leagues work?

ESPN (public and private) and Sleeper sync automatically. Yahoo is waiting on Yahoo's approval. Any other site works by entering your roster by hand.

Will it change my lineup?

No. It can only read your league. Alerts tell you exactly what to swap, and you make the move in your league's app.

Where does the injury news come from?

Public NFL injury reports and team depth charts, checked every 2 minutes within 3 hours of a game and every 15 minutes otherwise. Game-day inactives usually arrive within minutes of the official list, about 90 minutes before kickoff.

What do you do with my information?

Only what's needed to send your alerts. Nothing is sold. Read the privacy policy.
