Contact

Questions, bug reports and ideas are all welcome. Email is the best way to reach us:

support@fantasyinjuryassist.com

You'll usually get a reply within a day or two, faster during the season.

Helpful details to include

  • The email address you sign in with.
  • Which device and browser you use, or whether you're in the Android app.
  • For a missing or wrong alert: the player, the team and roughly when it happened.
  • For a linked league: whether it's ESPN or Sleeper, and whether the ESPN league is private.

Common questions

Not getting notifications?
Open the app's Settings tab and tap Test next to notifications. On an iPhone, alerts only work after you add the site to your home screen and open it from there.
Want to delete your account?
Settings → Delete account removes your account and everything tied to it right away. You can also email us and we'll do it for you.
Spotted a wrong injury status?
Statuses come straight from official team reports and usually update within minutes. If one still looks wrong after a refresh, please email us with the player's name.
